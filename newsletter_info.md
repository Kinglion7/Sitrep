# Newsletter checklist — collect this before opening the editor

Two weeks of content, five pages. Everything below maps to a field in the
editor. Keep names and events releasable: the newsletter is UNCLASSIFIED, for
families, but the file travels.

## Issue facts
- Publication date (the Friday you send it)
- Training weeks covered, e.g. `TW51–TW52`
- Issue number (the editor advances it for you)

## Cover
- **Layout**: 1a Classic (one strong photo with a face), 1b Banded (landscape
  or group photo, three teasers), 1c Vertical (subject on the right, big week
  numerals), 1d Paper (tall portrait, contents list on the cover)
- **Photo**: the best single frame of the period. Landscape ≥ 2000 px wide.
  Face or action in the middle third; the masthead covers the top 25 % on 1a.
- **Headline**: 2–4 words (1d) or up to 6 words (others)
- **Subhead**: one sentence
- **Top Wolf teaser** (1a): `RANK Lastname — why`
- **Three coverlines** (1b): tag + one line each
- **Kicker** (1c): `WEEK n · EVENT NAME`
- **Contents blurbs** (1d): one short line per section

## p.2 — Commander's Summary
- Headline (3–5 words) and an italic deck sentence
- Body: 120–180 words, two or three paragraphs. More overflows into the
  Forward Ops block; the preview outlines it in red.
- *Evaluated / trained this period*: 4–7 short items
- Pull quote: one sentence, optional

## p.2 — Forward Ops
- Who is away and with which task force (as releasable)
- 2–3 sentences; photo of them or their site, roughly square

## p.3 — In the Field
- Up to six photos, landscape preferred, each with a LABEL and one-line
  caption. Fewer photos is fine: the grid tightens to 4 or 2.
- Mix: training, people, equipment, one candid

## p.4 — Command Asks + Priorities
- Division assistance: `NSTR` or a short ask + one sentence
- DSB assistance: short ask + one or two sentences on what it enables
- Four priorities for the next training weeks, a few words each, optional
  note such as `(MET)`

## p.5 — Top Wolf
- Rank and last name
- Three stats: a big word or number each (`SPC→SGT`, `NCOIC`, `OPSEC`) with a
  four-word caption
- Two paragraphs, 120–160 words total
- Portrait photo, tall, chest up, subject centred (the crop is 360 × 1056)

## Email version
- Intro line, closing line, contact line (SFRG lead name and how to reach them)

## Before you publish
- Every grey photo box filled or its caption cleared
- No red outlines in the preview
- Names, ranks and dates checked against the roster
- PDF exported and opened once (5 pages, no blank page at the end)
- Passphrase unchanged from last issue unless you told the families
